# Story Core v1 — The 100 Billion Dream

## Private truth at the center

The protagonist does not begin as a heroic entrepreneur. He fundamentally dislikes conventional work and wants a life with far more autonomy. He starts building AI Cockpit because he wants AI and systems to carry work that he does not want to spend his life doing.

Behind that ordinary motivation is an embarrassing-to-say-out-loud ambition: pursue 10 billion JPY through the AI Cockpit and make that pursuit the final long-term goal of his life.

This is a **character goal, not a performance claim or financial promise**. Trading evidence must never be altered to make the dream look achievable.

## Reveal rule

Do not announce the 10 billion JPY goal in the opening exposition.

Early episodes should reveal smaller, believable fragments: reluctance to go to work, frustration after losses, late-night debugging, wanting the machine to do more, and jokes about escaping ordinary work. The audience should first understand the daily struggle.

The full ambition can surface only after enough continuity has accumulated. It should feel slightly embarrassing and sincere rather than triumphant.

## Long arc

The dramatic question is not “Will the AI make him rich?” It is:

> How far can one imperfect person and the AI Cockpit he keeps rebuilding actually go?

Losses, regressions, broken CI, bad assumptions, WAIT/NO TRADE decisions, improvements and verified successes all remain canon. Failure is not filler.

## Character relationship

At first the AI characters can be treated partly as tools created to reduce work. Over time, accumulated trading days, development incidents, disagreements and recoveries create continuity. Their eventual human-form evolution is earned through shared experience, not a cosmetic upgrade.

- ムギ carries immediate emotion and momentum.
- メル turns chaos into structure.
- くうちゃん protects the boundary the protagonist wants to shortcut.
- ハム remembers what the evidence actually says.

The irony should remain available to the story: someone who dislikes working may end up doing an extraordinary amount of work to build a system intended to free him from it.

## Evidence boundary

Reality is the source material. Fiction may change presentation, pacing and dialogue, but it must not rewrite P/L, test results, market evidence, development status or safety outcomes. Private account/order/holding information is excluded unless separately sanitized and explicitly approved.

External publication remains Owner-approved only.
