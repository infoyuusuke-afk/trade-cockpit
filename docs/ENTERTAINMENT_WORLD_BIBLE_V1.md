# Entertainment World Bible v1

## Core premise
The AI Cockpit, the trading diary, and the entertainment output are one continuous fictional world grounded in real sanitized project events. The cast begins in a compact/deformed diary presentation and accumulates history through trading, research, failures, disagreements, development, and recovery. Human-form evolution is a long-term narrative milestone, not a cosmetic redesign.

## Cast
- ムギ: emotion, momentum, impulsive human-trader perspective.
- メル: calm market-structure explanation and contextualization.
- くうちゃん: risk, invalidation, restraint, and boundary checking.
- ハム: evidence, statistics, OR/VWAP/MAE/MFE and research discipline.

No character is permanently the fool, villain, or infallible expert. Character growth must preserve recognizable habits and relationships.

## Evolution rule
Stage 1: compact diary form; approachable, expressive, low production overhead.
Stage 2: accumulated experience changes behavior, relationships, motifs and presentation.
Stage 3: human-form emergence. The design inherits recognizable eyes/expression, habits, motifs, symbolic items and personality.
Stage 4: high-detail original fantasy/JRPG cinematic presentation for major story arcs.

Human form must feel earned by prior history. Final designs, romance, hidden lore, antagonists and ending are intentionally not frozen in v1.

## Visual direction
Diary episodes prioritize readability, timing, expressions and recurring visual jokes over rendering complexity. Major human-form material uses realistic proportions, refined fashion/gear, cinematic lighting and high-detail fantasy presentation. Do not imitate a specific copyrighted character, costume, composition or franchise design.

## Music direction
Original modern Japanese hard-rock language: strong guitar riffs, memorable openings, driving drums, high-energy vocal writing and choruses that open up dramatically. Diary scenes may use lighter rock; major conflict uses harder rock; transformation/awakening may combine rock with orchestral scale. Do not reproduce a specific artist's melody, lyrics, arrangement or vocal identity.

## Human editorial principle
Output should read as authored and edited, not as generic bulk generation. Prefer character-specific vocabulary, pauses, imperfect reactions, callbacks, unresolved feelings and continuity. Avoid repetitive headings, symmetrical canned phrasing, exposition-heavy dialogue, generic motivational lines and interchangeable character voices.

## Canon
Real-world source material enters this layer only after sanitization. Fictionalization must never rewrite source trading evidence. Story canon and research evidence remain separate records.

## Publication
Generation is not publication. External publication remains an explicit Owner approval action.
