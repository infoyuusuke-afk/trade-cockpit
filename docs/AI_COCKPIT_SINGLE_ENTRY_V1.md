# AI Cockpit Single Entry v1

AI Cockpit is the sole user-facing entrance.

Trading research, Japan-market daily coverage, developer growth, diary, Entertainment Department and overseas-community research are internal subsystems. They must not require the Owner to think in terms of separate GPT tasks or separate products.

The router is intentionally side-effect free. It selects a subsystem but cannot place an order or publish externally.

Conceptual flow:

Owner → AI Cockpit → internal subsystem → evidence/result → AI Cockpit presentation.

The Japan-market daily lane remains independent of whether the Owner traded. Entertainment can consume sanitized diary/development events. Global community feedback remains quarantined until independently verified.

External publication and any future real-submit capability remain separate approval boundaries.
