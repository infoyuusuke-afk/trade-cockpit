# Multi-horizon Strategy Research

Strategy Lab covers INTRADAY, SWING and POSITION horizons.

Candlestick patterns, Dow-style market structure and Elliott-wave hypotheses are research families, not trusted signals. Subjective concepts must first become explicit reproducible variants: pivot definition, confirmation, invalidation, entry, exit, costs and look-ahead-safe timestamps.

Research should test conditional expectancy by symbol class, regime and horizon. Combinations are allowed, but must survive out-of-sample or walk-forward validation to reduce overfitting. External trader methods receive no reputation-based privilege.

Promotion remains governed by Strategy Evolution and the upper autonomy permission gate. This registry never grants broker submission.
