# Global Japan-Market Daily — Editorial Contract v1

The daily product should be useful to a trader who follows Japan from outside Japan, not merely readable English.

Each internal brief carries five blocks: index context, market leaders, themes, notable moves, and unknowns. Claims are typed as OBSERVED, COMPUTED, ATTRIBUTED, INTERPRETATION, or UNKNOWN. Factual/attributed claims require evidence references.

The Global edition must add relevant Japan-specific context rather than mechanically translating the JP edition. When evidence does not establish why a stock or sector moved, the reason stays UNKNOWN.

This gate prepares an internal draft only. Passing it does not authorize publication.
