# Developer Growth → Entertainment

Developer growth is no longer a separate user-facing daily entrance. It is source material inside AI Cockpit.

Flow:

AI Cockpit development evidence → verified/sanitized growth event → Entertainment Department → manga/X/YouTube internal brief → Owner approval → optional publication.

The story should dramatize concrete development moments: a failed CI check, a wrong assumption, debugging, a safety stop, a design improvement, or a successful merge. Do not fabricate competence or infer progress from activity volume.

Growth is deliberately small-grained (-1/0/+1 per verified event), so the narrative can show setbacks and plateaus instead of turning every day into artificial improvement.

Character use:
- ムギ carries the immediate emotional reaction.
- ハム checks logs/evidence.
- くうちゃん challenges unsafe shortcuts.
- メル turns the incident into a reusable design lesson.

The developer arc and trading arc belong to the same world, but evidence remains separate from fiction. Private paths, credentials, account/order/holding data and unsanitized records never enter entertainment input. External publication remains disabled until explicit Owner approval.
