# Editorial Quality Guard v1

This is an editorial lint layer, not an AI-authorship detector.

Its job is to catch obvious template drift before an internal Entertainment brief reaches human editing: duplicate titles, repeated openings, canned phrases and an unsafe publication state.

Passing the lint does **not** mean content is good or human-authored. Final quality still depends on continuity, specific real events, distinct character voices, imperfect reactions and Owner editorial judgment.

The guard must never rewrite trading evidence merely to improve a story.
