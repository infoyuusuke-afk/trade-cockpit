# AI Cockpit Daily Orchestration v1

A daily cycle must not depend on the Owner being able or willing to trade.

When verified Japan-market evidence exists, MARKET_DAILY remains planned even on a no-trade day. Personal trade analysis is added only when personal trade evidence exists. Verified development work or a sanitized diary can independently create Entertainment candidates.

If market evidence is missing, the market lane reports WAIT_DATA rather than inventing a recap.

This planner has no scheduler, order submission or external publishing capability. It describes what AI Cockpit should prepare; activation and publication remain separate controlled boundaries.
