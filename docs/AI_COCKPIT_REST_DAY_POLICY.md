# AI Cockpit Rest / No-Trade Day Policy v1

The Owner being unavailable, taking a rest day, or choosing not to trade must not be treated as a system failure.

On such a day:
- no personal trade is expected and no negative score is created for inactivity;
- verified Japan-market daily work should continue;
- existing sanitized development/diary material may still feed internal Entertainment work;
- missing personal observations are not fabricated;
- sensitive external publication and any future real-submit path remain separately controlled.

The goal is operational resilience, not pressure to trade.
